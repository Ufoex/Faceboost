# Fork changes (Ufoex/Faceboost, formerly Materialbook)

Fork of [eepiemi/Materialbook](https://github.com/eepiemi/Materialbook) that combines the useful work of other forks.
All credit to eepiemi and to the fork authors below. Package: `com.ufoex.materialbook` (installs next to the original).

| Source | What was taken |
|---|---|
| [ofirc73/AstryxBook](https://github.com/ofirc73/AstryxBook) | Picture-in-Picture for reels with selectable ratios, lock-screen audio, HTML5 fullscreen video, portrait lock on phones (except fullscreen video), auto-desktop fixes, concurrent script fetch with timeout, tests |
| [dh6k/Materialbook_fork](https://github.com/dh6k/Materialbook_fork) | Open Messenger links in the Messenger app (configurable package), external links in the default browser, hardened sponsored-post detection, photo viewer fixes, feed slowdown fix (bundle inject-once guard), lighter back-navigation, hide "Open app" banner |
| [farhun1/BraveBook](https://github.com/farhun1/BraveBook) | Network-level ad/tracker blocking with the Brave block list (toggle in settings), third-party cookies off, thread-safe toasts, hide group posts from the feed |
| [Kdomy/LiteBook](https://github.com/Kdomy/LiteBook) | Copy post text on long press, reliable photo/video/reel downloads (video+audio mux), legible AMOLED login screens, `lm.facebook.com` redirects, VIEW intents while the app is open |
| [tachiyuu/Materialbook](https://github.com/tachiyuu/Materialbook) | The idea and the label list of hiding AI-generated posts (rewritten here as a standalone script, mobile site only) |

Own additions in this repo: a collapsing toolbar (top bars hide when scrolling down and come back when scrolling up; needs Sticky Navbar), reel controls (time, seek bar, fullscreen and volume on paused reels, a button that hides all the reel UI except the video and its progress line, and a button that switches between filling the screen and showing the whole video), movable reel buttons (hold one to edit; they snap to a grid, with Save and Cancel), auto-scroll for reels (setting; goes to the next reel when the current one ends, with a button in the reel viewer to pause it for the session), reel gestures (setting; hold to play faster, double tap left or right to skip a chosen number of seconds), a Resize videos setting (the whole video without cropping, with a button to switch it off temporarily), feedback icons and fade transitions when toggling the reel buttons, reel audio with the screen off (setting; adds a button in the reel viewer, off by default, that keeps the reel's audio playing when the screen turns off), a lighter reel ad scan that also covers every video viewer, a reel viewer that stays on the reel you are watching when Facebook rebuilds the list (and fades the thumbnail into the video when it starts), an update check on start with a Check now button in Settings (looks at this repo's latest GitHub release and offers the APK), launcher shortcuts, a notifications row and first-launch prompt, a Hide navigation bar setting, sensor rotation for fullscreen video, the Faceboost name and icon, translations of the settings, the Messages in desktop mode setting, links to other sites in a Custom Tab (setting), keep screen on (setting), export and import of the settings, text size, a Messages layer that rotates and offers PiP, an app lock (confirmed with your fingerprint or PIN when you turn it on), a Share via row in Facebook's share sheet, page scripts that always come from inside the app (never downloaded), Messenger calls with a camera and microphone permission flow, log out and share-page rows, Back that keeps the app in the background, and hardening ideas from AstryxBook rewritten here (page bridges limited to Facebook's main frame among them): session-free backups, no third-party cookies, a pinned WebView, downloads limited to Facebook's main frame and to photos and videos, a confirmation before a page opens another app, validated start links and cleaner shared links.

Messages in desktop mode was first written here and then merged into AstryxBook (PR #6), whose maintainer reworked it: Messages opens in its own desktop WebView layer over the feed, so the feed keeps its scroll position. This fork uses that version, and keeps the setting on by default.

Not taken: the other forks' rebrands, Astryx's/dh6k's overlapping orientation and auto-desktop logic, BraveBook's own fullscreen manager (Astryx's is used),
LiteBook's `MIXED_CONTENT_ALWAYS_ALLOW` (insecure).

Seen in the forks after the last merge (checked 2026-10-14, not taken yet):

- AstryxBook: lock the app when the screen turns off, block screenshots, clear the cache on leaving, start on Feeds; text size as a page zoom that survives page loads, with an optional text wrap when zoomed in; Facebook notifications while the app is closed, optionally showing what they say; sharing photos and videos from other apps into Facebook's composer, with the share entries named and ordered like the Facebook app (Your story, Feed, Your groups, Reels) and Set as profile picture; the WebView is replaced when its renderer process is lost; the Open app bar under a post's photos is hidden; the story creator's preview shows the whole story on a phone; RTL settings gears; reels downloaded from the player's MP4; the fork's strings translated into every supported language.
- Taken from AstryxBook on 2026-10-27: sponsored reels labelled in non-Latin scripts, the ProGuard rule for WorkManager (R8), PiP staying on the video after a long lock, Facebook's Google Ads pixel blocked (written in `BlockingWebViewClient`) and the automatic dismissal of the "better on the app" sheet. Its newer PiP rework (close an empty PiP window, keep a resized window's size and zoom), the Messages Back fixes, the story editor and the memories fixes are not taken: they depend on an AstryxBook `MainActivity` restructure this fork does not have.
- LiteBook: removes the stuck full-screen loading overlay that blocked the account switch screen.
- Upstream (eepiemi/Materialbook): a LICENSE update.
- dh6k and BraveBook: nothing new.

Page scripts are never downloaded: the app always runs the copies bundled in it (`bundleScripts` in `fetchScripts.kt`). Only the ad block list is still fetched from this repo's `main`, with a bundled fallback.
